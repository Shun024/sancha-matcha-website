Our Story

From a hillside in Uji to a corner of London.

Sancha began with a simple obsession: matcha that tastes the way it should — vivid, grassy-sweet, never bitter. We work directly with a family tea garden in Uji, the birthplace of Japanese matcha, stone-milling small batches so the leaf never sits, never dulls.

No powders bulked with sugar. No neon colouring. Just shade-grown tencha, whisked by hand and served with the calm the ritual deserves.
