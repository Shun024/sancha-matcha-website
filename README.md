# Sancha Matcha — Website Concept Deck

Three complete, self-contained homepage concepts for **Sancha Matcha**, a matcha drink
store in London ([@sanchamatchaa](https://www.instagram.com/sanchamatchaa)).

## How to view

Open **`index.html`** (the deck landing page) in any browser — it links to all three
concepts. Or open each concept directly:

| Concept | Folder | Vibe |
|---|---|---|
| 01 — Zen Minimal | `style-1-zen/index.html` | Elegant, ceremonial, calm |
| 02 — Vibrant Modern | `style-2-vibrant/index.html` | Bold, playful, high-energy |
| 03 — Warm Earthy Artisan | `style-3-earthy/index.html` | Cosy, editorial, hand-made |

Each file is a single HTML page with embedded CSS/SVG and Google Fonts — no build step,
no dependencies. Just double-click. (Internet needed only for the web fonts.)

## Research behind the designs

**The store (confirmed from Instagram).** The public profile header for
[@sanchamatchaa](https://www.instagram.com/sanchamatchaa) reads
**"SANcha | Pop-ups | Private Events | Corporate Events"** — so SANcha is a **roaming /
pop-up matcha bar and events caterer in London, not a fixed café chain.** Everything else
(menu, prices, pop-up schedule, contact) sits behind Instagram's login wall and isn't
indexed elsewhere, so the drinks/pricing below are **realistic placeholders** modelled on
London matcha bars — swap in the real details. All three sites reflect the pop-up/events
model: a live **pop-up schedule** plus a **private & corporate event booking** section
instead of fixed store locations. (Note: `@drinksancha` and `@sancha.matcha` are different
accounts — confirm menu direct from `@sanchamatchaa`.)

**Confirmed pop-up pitches (now live in all three sites):**
- **Westfield White City**, W12 7GF — Mon–Sat 10–9, Sun 12–6
- **Backyard Market** (Brick Lane), E1 6QL — Sat 11–6, Sun 10–6
- **Battersea River Walk Market**, SW11 8DD — Sun 11–5

Still placeholder (awaiting real data): drink names & prices, event booking email.

**Reference sites studied:**

- **Matchado (matchado.co.uk)** — Premium Japanese matcha, Great Taste Award winner,
  strong baked-goods range (canelé, sablé, loaf cake), four London cafés. Natural greens +
  neutral tones; sophisticated but approachable. *→ informed the product/menu content.*
- **SMATCHA London** — "Ceremonial Grade… balance, clarity, everyday ritual." Minimalist,
  neutral/earthy, clean e-commerce structure (About / Shop / Loyalty / Locations).
  *→ informed the Zen concept and the retail kit pricing.*
- **Jenki** — Dark matcha green + white/black, spacious grid, award credentials,
  subscription push, "matcha vs coffee" education, physical bars. *→ informed the
  benefits/"why matcha" section in the Vibrant concept.*
- **How Matcha** — Cream backgrounds, bold statement headlines ("ENERGY FLOWS IN YOU"),
  product-grid + lifestyle photography, wellness tone. *→ informed the bold typography and
  marquee treatments.*

**2026 design trends applied:** oversized/kinetic typography and storytelling headlines;
warm luxurious earth tones alongside the expected wellness greens; a Y2K-adjacent
saturated palette option; generous whitespace and rounded, tactile UI. (Sources: Figma web
design trends, Lummi/Envato/Vistaprint 2026 colour trends, matcha-branding guides.)

## The three directions

### 01 · Zen Minimal — ★ SELECTED & BUILT WITH REAL CONTENT
- **Palette:** olive matcha `#6c7a55`, deep olive `#4c5541`, forest `#28331a` (both
  sampled from the real logo), cream `#f5f2e9`, stone `#b6a892`, charcoal `#26271f`.
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond (serif display) + Jost (sans body).
- **Feel:** ceremonial, premium, quiet.
- **Now includes the real brand** (from the supplied PDFs):
  - Hand-drawn SANcha logo — transparent PNGs in `style-1-zen/assets/` (dark for light
    sections, cream for dark, olive emblem for the hero, + favicon), converted from the
    supplied logo PDFs.
  - Real founder story (three friends, coffee → matcha) + the **三茶 / "three teas"** name
    meaning + **Zhejiang, China** sourcing.
  - **Tinned matcha as hero product: IKI (粋)** signature blend (Longjing 43 + Yabukita)
    and **YUAN (源)** single-cultivar Yabukita, with tasting notes. 30g now, 500g flagged
    as coming.
  - Matcha-vs-coffee **benefits**, a **Shop** (tins / merch / gift cards), **B2B wholesale**
    (500g & 1kg online, 10kg+ enquiry), and the **Westfield Shepherd's Bush flagship** +
    weekend pop-ups.
  - **No drinks menu** (per the brief). Prices shown as "coming soon" until confirmed.

### 02 · Vibrant Modern
- **Palette:** electric lime `#9ede3f`, deep forest `#0f2e1d`, cream `#fdfbef`, peach
  accent `#ff9d7a`.
- **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque (heavy display) + Space Grotesk (body).
- **Feel:** loud, playful, social-first. Pill nav, tilted marquee, animated cup, colour-
  block cards. Best for a young, Instagram/TikTok-driven audience and viral iced-matcha
  culture.

### 03 · Warm Earthy Artisan
- **Palette:** clay `#c07a4c`, umber `#6b4a34`, olive `#7c8034`, warm cream `#f3e9db`,
  gold `#c9a24b`.
- **Type:** Fraunces (serif display) + Work Sans (body).
- **Feel:** cosy neighbourhood café, editorial, hand-made. Rotating seal badge, ticker,
  "pantry" shop grid, founder signature. Best if bakes + a warm in-store experience are
  central to the brand.

## Shared structure (all three)
Fixed nav → hero → moving marquee/ticker → story/about → benefits or pillars →
signature drinks menu → retail shop → **"Catch us next" pop-up schedule → Private &
Corporate event booking** (with `mailto:` enquiry) → newsletter capture → footer with
Instagram link. Fully responsive (mobile breakpoints included).
