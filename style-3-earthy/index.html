Our House

A little house built around one cup.

Sancha Matcha started at a market stall with a bamboo whisk and a stubborn belief: matcha should taste warm and generous, not bitter and thin. We found our leaf on a single family hillside in Uji — the historic home of Japanese matcha — and never looked back.

Everything else is made to match: canelés and loaf cakes baked each morning, plant milks that don't fight the tea, and cups poured with a bit of patience. Come sit a while.

— Hiro & the Sancha team
What we care about

Three things, done properly.

Single origin

One family garden in Uji, one cultivar, stone-milled in small batches so it's never dull.

Baked each morning

Matcha canelés, sablés and loaf cakes — no artificial colour, no shortcuts, just butter and care.

Unhurried by design

Markets, weddings or the office — wherever we land, we bring the calm with us. Real ceremony, on the move.
