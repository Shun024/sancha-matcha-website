Introduction

Welcome to SANcha. At SANcha, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect your personal information when you visit our website, purchase our product, communicate with us or otherwise interact with our business.

We process personal information in accordance with applicable UK data protection legislation, including:

  • The UK General Data Protection Regulation (“UK GDPR”);
  • The Data Protection Act 2018, as amended;
  • The Privacy and Electronic Communications (EC Directive) Regulations 2003 (“PECR”), as amended; and
  • The Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, insofar as its relevant provisions are in force.

By using our website, you acknowledge that you have been provided with this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by law, we will request it separately.

How and Why We Use Your Personal Information

SANcha processes personal information only where there is a lawful basis for doing so under Article 6 of the UK GDPR. We are committed to ensuring that all personal information is processed lawfully, fairly and transparently, and that such processing is limited to what is necessary for legitimate and specified purposes.

We primarily process your personal information to facilitate the operation of our website and online store, including processing purchases, administering payments, arranging deliveries, managing customer accounts and providing order confirmations and updates. Where processing is necessary to fulfil an order or provide a service you have requested, we rely on the lawful basis of performance of a contract under Article 6(1)(b) of the UK GDPR.

We may also process your personal information to respond to customer enquiries, manage complaints, provide customer support and administer returns, exchanges and refunds. Depending on the circumstances, such processing may be necessary for the performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation or the pursuit of our legitimate interests in maintaining effective customer relationships and delivering a reliable service.

Where you contact SANcha regarding wholesale matcha supply, bulk purchases, commercial partnerships or other business opportunities, we process the information you provide to assess your requirements, prepare quotations, communicate product specifications, negotiate commercial arrangements and establish or maintain business relationships. Such processing is generally undertaken on the basis of our legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) of the UK GDPR. Where processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract with you personally, or to perform such a contract, we may instead rely on Article 6(1)(b).

We process certain personal information to comply with our statutory and regulatory obligations, including maintaining financial records, fulfilling accounting and taxation requirements, and responding to lawful requests from regulatory authorities. In these circumstances, we rely on the lawful basis of compliance with a legal obligation under Article 6(1)(c) of the UK GDPR.

We may process technical information and website usage data to maintain the security, functionality and performance of our website, prevent fraudulent transactions, identify potential security threats and improve our products and services. Where appropriate, we rely on our legitimate interests in protecting our business, customers and digital infrastructure. The use of cookies and similar tracking technologies remains subject to applicable requirements under PECR, as amended.

Where you have subscribed to receive promotional communications, we may use your contact information to provide updates about SANcha's products, merchandise, events, promotions and wholesale opportunities. We will rely on your consent under Article 6(1)(a) of the UK GDPR where required, or on our legitimate interests where direct marketing is otherwise permitted by applicable law, including the relevant requirements of PECR. You may withdraw your consent or object to direct marketing at any time.

We may additionally process personal information where reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, protect our legal rights or respond to disputes. Such processing will be undertaken on an appropriate lawful basis, including our legitimate interests or compliance with a legal obligation, depending on the circumstances.

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we will assess and balance those interests against your rights, freedoms and reasonable expectations to ensure that the processing is lawful and proportionate. We will not process your personal information for purposes that are incompatible with those for which it was originally collected unless such further processing is permitted or required by applicable law.

How Long We Keep Your Personal Information

SANcha is committed to ensuring that personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected and processed, in accordance with the storage limitation principle under Article 5(1)(e) of the UK GDPR. The period for which we retain personal information depends on its nature, the purposes for which it is processed, our contractual and commercial requirements, and any applicable statutory, regulatory, accounting or legal obligations. We periodically review the personal information held by us to ensure that it remains relevant, accurate and necessary.

Personal information relating to purchases, transactions, invoices, payments and other financial records will generally be retained for six years, or such other period as may be required by applicable taxation, accounting or company law.

Where you create a customer account on our website, we may retain your account information for as long as the account remains active or as otherwise reasonably necessary to provide the associated services. Following account closure or a prolonged period of inactivity, we will review the information held and securely delete or anonymise information that is no longer required, subject to any applicable legal or legitimate retention requirements.

Personal information collected through customer service communications, complaints, returns, refund requests and other enquiries will be retained for the period reasonably necessary to address the relevant matter, maintain appropriate records of our communications and, where applicable, establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Information provided in connection with wholesale enquiries, commercial negotiations, bulk orders and business supply arrangements may be retained for the duration of the relevant business relationship and for a reasonable period thereafter. Where a prospective business relationship does not proceed, we will retain the relevant information only for a defined and proportionate period consistent with the original purpose of collection and our legitimate business interests.

Where you have subscribed to receive marketing communications, we will retain the relevant contact information and marketing preferences for as long as you remain subscribed and the information remains necessary for that purpose. If you withdraw consent or unsubscribe, we will cease the relevant marketing activities and may retain limited information in a suppression record to ensure that your preferences are respected.

Technical information, website usage data and information collected through cookies or similar technologies will be retained in accordance with the applicable purposes, technical settings and retention periods of the relevant technologies. In certain circumstances, we may retain personal information for longer periods where necessary to comply with a legal obligation, respond to regulatory investigations, resolve disputes or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Any such extended retention will be limited to what is necessary and proportionate.

Once personal information is no longer required for the purposes for which it was collected, and there is no lawful basis for its continued retention, we will take appropriate steps to securely delete, anonymise or otherwise dispose of the information. SANcha will not retain personal information indefinitely without a legitimate and lawful reason.

Your Rights Under UK Data Protection Law

Subject to applicable legal conditions and exemptions, you have the following rights in relation to your personal information.

  • Right of Access: You may request confirmation of whether we process your personal information and obtain access to that information.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate personal information or completion of incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request deletion of your personal information in circumstances where the right to erasure applies. This right is not absolute, and we may need to retain certain information to comply with legal obligations or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You may request that we restrict the processing of your personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability: Where applicable, you may request to receive personal information you have provided to us in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, or request its transmission to another controller where technically feasible.
  • Right to Object: You may object to processing based on legitimate interests in certain circumstances. You also have the right to object at any time to the processing of your personal information for direct marketing purposes.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.
  • Rights Relating to Automated Decision-Making: Where applicable, you may have rights and protections relating to decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

SANcha does not currently intend to use customer information to make solely automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on individuals. If this changes, we will provide the information and safeguards required by applicable law.
