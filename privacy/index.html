Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

This policy is a plain-English starting point covering how the SANcha website currently works. Please have it reviewed and add your registered business details before relying on it commercially.

This Privacy Policy explains how SANcha ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects your personal information when you visit sanchamatcha.co.uk or get in touch with us. We are based in London, United Kingdom.

Information we collect

  • Contact & enquiries. If you email us (for example a wholesale or bulk enquiry, or to be notified at launch), we receive your email address and anything you choose to include in your message.
  • Newsletter sign-up. If you ask to be notified about launches and updates, we collect the email address you provide for that purpose.
  • Technical basics. Our host may process standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to serve the site securely. We do not use these to identify you.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide the information you asked for.
  • To send you updates you have signed up for. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

Legal basis (UK GDPR)

We process your information on the basis of your consent (for marketing updates you opt into) and our legitimate interests (to respond to enquiries and run the website). You can withdraw consent at any time.

Cookies & analytics

This website does not currently set marketing or analytics cookies. If we add website analytics in future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with service providers who help us run the site and communicate with you — for example our website host (GitHub Pages) and our email provider — and where the law requires it.

Keeping your information

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes above, then delete it.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or object to our use of your personal information, to restrict processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these, email contact@sanchamatcha.co.uk. If you have a concern you can also contact the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email contact@sanchamatcha.co.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.
