Returns & exchanges

We accept returns only for goods that arrive faulty or damaged. To process your request, please attach clear photos and/or a short video showing the fault or damage. Our team will be in touch with the next steps. Full details are in our Terms of Sale.

Required — returns are only accepted for faulty or damaged goods. Attach clear photos and/or a short video clip. Up to 5 files, max 20 MB each (please keep videos short).
