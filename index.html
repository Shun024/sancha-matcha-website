Our Story

Three friends, one better cup.

We're three professionals who made an unconventional decision — we left our corporate careers to build something we genuinely believe in.

Like most people around us, we ran on coffee: quick energy, followed by crashes, jitters and burnout. A cycle we couldn't seem to escape. So we went looking for something better and found matcha — a cleaner, more sustained source of caffeine that supports focus without the spike, thanks to its natural pairing of caffeine and L‑theanine.

But it was never just about energy. Rich in antioxidants and gentler on the body than acidic coffee, matcha turned a personal shift into something bigger. We realised that experienced this way, matcha isn't just an alternative — it becomes a daily ritual.

Where our matcha comes from

Chinese roots, refined craft.

We work directly with producers in Zhejiang, China, using modern processing techniques inspired by Japanese methods — finely milled, high-grade matcha with the consistency to match.

Matcha is often tied to Japan, but its origins trace back to China, where powdered tea was first developed; Japan later refined it into the matcha we know today. Our goal is to bring traditional knowledge together with modern quality control, for a consistent, high-quality cup every time.
