Our Story

Good matcha shouldn't have to come at a high price.

Finding good-quality matcha in the UK at an affordable price isn't always easy. As matcha became more popular, we started thinking about why it had to be this way — and whether there was another way to enjoy good matcha without paying more and more for it.

Japan has long been at the heart of matcha, and as demand continues to grow, sourcing becomes increasingly competitive. We started wondering: what if we looked beyond Japan?

That question led us to China.

With a long history of tea cultivation of its own, China felt like a place worth exploring. So we travelled there, visited tea farms, learnt more about the people and culture behind the tea, and most importantly, tasted and tested the matcha ourselves.

We didn't want to choose a product based on its origin alone. We wanted to find matcha we genuinely enjoyed, that met our standards for quality and taste, and that we could bring to more people at a price that felt fair.

That's how SANcha began.

We're here to make good matcha more accessible, to keep exploring what matcha can be, and to share what we discover along the way.

Good matcha, more accessible. That's what SANcha is all about.
Where our matcha comes from

Chinese roots, refined craft.

We work directly with producers in Zhejiang, China, using modern processing techniques inspired by Japanese methods — finely milled, high-grade matcha with the consistency to match.

Matcha is often tied to Japan, but its origins trace back to China, where powdered tea was first developed; Japan later refined it into the matcha we know today. Our goal is to bring traditional knowledge together with modern quality control, for a consistent, high-quality cup every time.

Frequently asked questions

How do we choose our matcha?

You'll often see matcha labelled as “ceremonial grade”, but the term doesn't have one universally recognised grading standard across the industry. Rather than relying on a label, we look at what matters to us: the tea's origin, cultivar, harvest, colour, aroma and, most importantly, taste.

We travel to source our matcha and taste-test the products ourselves, looking for the right balance of flavour and quality for each of our blends. Good matcha goes beyond a grade — it's about understanding the tea and finding what tastes good in your cup.

Will matcha give me the caffeine jitters?

No. Matcha gives clean, steady energy: its caffeine is balanced by an amino acid called L-theanine, so you get calm focus without the spike and crash of coffee — no jitters, no 3pm slump.

How do I prepare matcha?

Sift about half a teaspoon (1–2g) into a bowl, add a little hot — not boiling — water, and whisk in a zig-zag until frothy. Top up with water or your milk of choice, hot or iced.
